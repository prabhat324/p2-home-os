THE PROBLEM

“Leave us a Google review” sounds easy. Writing one isn’t.

01

The blank-box problem

Customers know how they felt, but often don’t know how to turn that feeling into a useful paragraph.

02

Too much friction

A review request competes with everything else in a customer’s day. Every extra thought required lowers completion.

03

Thin reviews help less

“Great place!” is positive, but it doesn’t tell future customers what made the experience good.

HOW REVIEWMUSE WORKS

From “I should leave a review” to something meaningful in under a minute.

1

Share your ReviewMuse link

Use it in your existing email, SMS, receipt, QR code or follow-up workflow.

2

Customer describes the experience

They choose a 1–5 rating, tap what went well or poorly, and add anything specific they remember.

3

Local AI helps with the words

ReviewMuse drafts a review from only what the customer told it. They can edit, shorten or regenerate it.

4

Customer approves and continues to Google

The final text is copied and the official Google review page opens for the customer to post themselves.

BUILT DIFFERENTLY

We’re not trying to manufacture five-star reviews.

ReviewMuse is designed to make honest feedback easier to express. A one-star customer gets the same writing assistance and the same path to Google as a five-star customer.

The AI is instructed not to invent details, inflate sentiment or hide criticism. The customer sees and controls every word before anything leaves ReviewMuse.
